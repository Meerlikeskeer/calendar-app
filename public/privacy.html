Household Planner Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Household Planner is a private household organization app. It is intended only for people invited by the household administrator.

Information we store

The app stores the approved account username, password-derived authentication credentials, contact email address, phone number, task details, notes, calendar times, task assignments, and reminder preferences that household members enter.

How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate household members, show the shared calendar, assign and complete tasks, and deliver reminders when those features are enabled. Household Planner does not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Storage and security

Household data is stored with Convex. The native mobile app stores authentication session tokens in the device's protected secure storage. Data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS and WebSocket connections.

Sharing

Tasks and their notes are visible to signed-in members of the same household. Contact email addresses and phone numbers are limited to the account holder's personal settings and are not shown as a household directory.

Your choices

You can update your own phone number, email address, reminder settings, and password from Profile & reminders. Contact the household administrator to remove a household account or request deletion of its data.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact your household administrator.
